MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement is entered into between Acme Cloud Ltd ("Vendor") and Northwind Retail Inc ("Customer") on 1 September 2026.

1. Definitions

"Affiliate" means any entity that controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a party.
"Confidential Information" means all non-public information disclosed by one party to the other that is marked confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential.
"Customer Data" means all data, content and materials uploaded to the Services by or on behalf of Customer.
"Fees" means the amounts payable by Customer for the Services as set out in an Order Form.
"Services" means the hosted software services described in an Order Form.
"Order Form" means an ordering document signed by both parties that references this Agreement.

2. Services

2.1 Vendor shall provide the Services to Customer in accordance with the applicable Order Form and the Documentation.
2.2 Vendor may update the Services from time to time provided that no update materially reduces the core functionality of the Services during a Subscription Term.

3. Fees and Payment

3.1 Customer shall pay the Fees within thirty (30) days of the date of a valid invoice.
3.2 Fees are exclusive of taxes. Customer is responsible for all sales, use and value-added taxes other than taxes on Vendor's income.
3.3 Vendor may increase the Fees for any renewal Subscription Term by no more than five percent (5%) upon at least sixty (60) days' written notice prior to the end of the then-current Subscription Term.

4. Term and Termination

4.1 This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues until all Order Forms have expired or been terminated.
4.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement for material breach if the breach is not cured within thirty (30) days after written notice.
4.3 Customer may terminate this Agreement or any Order Form for convenience upon ninety (90) days' written notice to Vendor. Vendor shall refund any prepaid Fees for the unused portion of the Subscription Term.
4.4 Upon termination, Vendor shall make Customer Data available for export for thirty (30) days, after which Vendor shall delete Customer Data.

5. Customer Data and Security

5.1 Customer retains all right, title and interest in and to Customer Data.
5.2 Vendor shall implement and maintain administrative, physical and technical safeguards consistent with ISO 27001 to protect Customer Data.
5.3 Vendor shall notify Customer without undue delay and in any event within seventy-two (72) hours after becoming aware of a Security Incident affecting Customer Data.
5.4 Vendor shall process Customer Data only in accordance with Customer's documented instructions and the Data Processing Addendum.

6. Confidentiality

6.1 Each party shall protect the other party's Confidential Information with at least the same degree of care it uses for its own, and no less than reasonable care.
6.2 The obligations in this Section 6 survive for five (5) years after termination, except that obligations regarding trade secrets survive for so long as they remain trade secrets.
